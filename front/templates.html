Choosing one

Which template should you actually use?

Applying through a big ATS

Use Harvard Clean, US Classic or Tech Minimal. One column, standard headings, nothing decorative — the safest parse of all twenty.

Applying in Germany, Austria or Switzerland

DACH Lebenslauf uses the tabular date-left layout recruiters there expect. Europass EU is the safe EU-wide alternative.

Senior or executive roles

Executive Serif, UK Executive CV and Consulting Compact put profile and scope first, with the quiet typography those readers trust.

Design, marketing and product

Creative Sidebar, Silicon Valley Split and Startup Bold carry more personality while keeping the text layer clean and selectable.

First job or a career switch

Fresh Graduate puts education and projects above experience, so the page leads with what you do have.

Academic and research

Academic CV expects long lists — publications, teaching, grants — and stays readable past two pages.
