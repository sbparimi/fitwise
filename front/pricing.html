FAQ

Pricing questions

Is the free plan a trial?

No. The builder, all 20 templates, the 28-check ATS scanner, the cover letter studio, the job board and PDF/DOCX export are free with no time limit and no account. Pro exists for people applying daily who want unlimited AI tailoring and cloud sync.

Can I pay right now?

Not yet. Card payments are not switched on. Choosing a plan adds you to the early-access list, nothing is charged, and you keep using everything on the free plan meanwhile.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your account until you use them, and re-downloading something you already generated never costs another credit.

Is there a refund policy?

When payments open: 14 days, no questions, on any first subscription payment. Credit packs are refundable while unused.

Is there a student or jobseeker discount?

The free plan is the jobseeker plan — it is the whole builder. If you need Pro features and cost is the blocker, write to us through the contact page and we will sort something out.
