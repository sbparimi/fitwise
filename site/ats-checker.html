Your report lands here

28 checks across parsing, structure, contact details, impact, action verbs, keywords and readability — each with the specific fix.

What gets checked

All 28 checks, in seven groups

Parsing & Readability 5

Text extraction, file format, length in words, decorative glyphs and icon fonts, bullet characters.

Structure & Sections 5

Experience, Education, Skills and Summary headings, plus whether your headings use names parsers recognise.

Contact Information 4

Email, phone, city/country and a LinkedIn URL — all as body text, not trapped in a page header or an image.

Impact & Metrics 4

Share of bullets carrying a number, concrete scale figures, bullet length and first-person pronouns.

Action Verbs & Language 3

Bullets opening on a strong verb, filler and cliché phrases, and repetition across your roles.

Keyword Alignment 4

Job-ad keyword coverage, named hard skills, the advertised title, and acronyms spelled out once.

Length & Readability 3

Estimated page count, average sentence length and date-format consistency across roles.
