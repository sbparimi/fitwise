Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This page explains what Fitwise collects when you use it, why, how long it's kept, and how to have it deleted. It describes how the service actually works today.

In short

  • To use the app pages (jobs, job pages, the tailoring tool, events, pricing) you sign in with Google. We store your Google account ID, email and name to create your free account.
  • The CV you tailor (the file you upload, or the text you paste) is stored on our server for 90 days, then deleted automatically. You can delete it, or your whole account, any time on your Your data page. The job ad text is not saved.
  • We keep first-party usage statistics: which pages you open, how you arrived, your device type and actions such as Apply clicks and tailorings. No third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We keep your email, your credit balance and a small record of each tailoring (job title and company, plus a fingerprint of the result) so that a paid download matches what you previewed.
  • The generated cover letter is kept for 14 days so you can unlock it, then deleted.
  • Tailoring runs on our own rules engine. Your text is not sent to any AI service today.
  • No advertising trackers. We don't sell or share your data.

Where it's stored

Fitwise runs on Cloudflare (Workers and a D1 database). Data is processed and stored on Cloudflare's network. Job pages you link to are fetched from the job site to read the ad.

AI processing

Today, Fitwise does not send your CV to any AI provider. If that changes, we'll update this page first and say so in the tool before your text is sent.

Payments

Payments are not live yet. When they open, card details will be handled by the payment provider and never stored by Fitwise.

Cookies

Fitwise sets no advertising cookies and uses no third-party analytics. It sets two first-party cookies: a random visitor ID (fw_vid, 1 year) for its own usage statistics, and, when you sign in with Google, a sign-in cookie (a random token, no personal data in it, 30 days). It also uses your browser's local storage for the random browser ID and your email, as described above. Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google and follows Google's privacy policy.

Your rights

On your Your data page (after sign-in) you can delete your stored CVs, or delete your account and everything linked to it (stored CVs, credits and history, tailoring records, cover letters, usage records), at any time. You can also ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion from the contact page with the email you used. We delete it and confirm. If you're in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Fitwise is meant for job seekers aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Material changes will be announced on the site.
