FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need an account to use Fitwise?

No. The builder, the ATS checker, the templates, the cover letter studio and the job board all work with no account at all. Your resumes are saved in your own browser’s storage. Signing in with Google or a one-time email code is optional, and only adds cloud sync so the same resumes appear on your phone and laptop.

Where is my resume stored?

By default, in your browser — nothing leaves your device until you ask for an AI rewrite, run a scan you choose to share, or sign in for cloud sync. When you run an ATS scan we store that scan so its link can be reopened later. When you sign in, your resumes and letters are stored against your account so they sync. You can delete any of it from the dashboard.

Which ATS systems is this tested against?

The 28 checks are modelled on the published parsing behaviour of Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS and Taleo — the five that handle most of the hiring in Europe and the US. They cover the things those parsers actually break on: multi-column layouts, icon fonts, non-standard headings, contact details in page headers, tables and image-only PDFs.

Will the AI invent experience I don’t have?

It is instructed not to, and the built-in engine structurally cannot — it rewrites what you wrote rather than generating new claims. Where a bullet would be stronger with a number, you will see a [X%] placeholder for you to fill with the real figure, not an invented one.

Is the PDF export actually ATS-readable?

Yes. The PDF is printed from live HTML text, so every word is selectable, copyable text rather than an image of text. That is the single biggest cause of a 0% parse, and it is why Fitwise never exports a screenshot.

Which AI models power the writing features?

A cascade of free and open-source hosted models with automatic failover: if one provider is rate-limited or slow, the next takes the request within about eight seconds. If every provider is unavailable, Fitwise’s own deterministic editing engine answers instead, so no feature ever shows an error page.

Where do the jobs come from?

They are curated each morning from employer career pages and job boards across Europe, filtered to English-speaking roles, with visa sponsorship status stated per role from the employer’s own wording. Every card links to the original posting.
